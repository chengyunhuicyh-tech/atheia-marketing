## 📡 Channel doctrine

**Permanently IN — the operating spine:**

| Channel | Role |
|---|---|
| Instagram | Primary discovery + promotion. Carousels + Stories + Reels — does the work email can't. |
| BFT pop-ups | Highest-converting channel. Member-only events. Run when booked. |
| Shopify Email — flows | Welcome (3) · Abandoned cart (2) · Post-purchase (2). Trigger at high-intent moments — work even at 2% list health. |
| Shopify Email — broadcasts | Lean only (~2/month: sale launch + last call). Hygiene, not conversion. |
| Meta Ads — retargeting | Warm audience. The "email reminder" replacement — where conversions actually happen at our scale. |
| Judge.me | Automated review requests + social-proof harvesting. |

**Permanently OUT — don't re-propose these:**

| Cut | Why |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp marketing | PDPA overhead, not justified at our scale |
| Klaviyo | Shopify Email covers it |
| Heavy email cadence (6–8 broadcasts) | 2% open rate — diminishing returns |
| Manual review outreach | Judge.me automates it |
| Influencer seeding (gift-for-post) | Doesn't get posts in SG → replaced by paid UGC + commission affiliate |
| Cold Meta as a sales channel | Pixel lacks conversion volume at <30 orders — see Hard-won learnings below |

**Conditional / KIV — switch on when capacity or budget allows:**

- **UGC content creator** (paid, ~SGD $150–250 for 2–3 Reels) — solves "can't film ourselves."
- **Affiliate influencers** (gifted + 10–15% commission, unique codes) — once brand presence is stronger.
- **Blog / SEO** — defer until SEO foundation exists.
- **Editorial PR** — SG editorial calendars fill 2–3 months ahead; low-effort pitches only.

## 💰 Budget reality & guardrails

- **Monthly envelope:** ~SGD $215–345 confirmed, up to ~SGD $595 if UGC + affiliate are switched on. Planning anchors, not a strict ledger.
- **Meta is thin-spend by necessity.** At $4–7/day it's a **retargeting net, not a discovery engine.** Discovery comes from IG Stories + pop-up word-of-mouth — warmer audiences than cold ads.
- **Money goes to:** Meta retargeting + sale push, pop-up materials, free-shipping margin, conditional Reel boosting (only after 2,000+ organic views), conditional UGC.
- **Money doesn't go to:** cold prospecting at scale, paid PR, heavy tooling.

## 🧠 Hard-won learnings (the moat — do not relearn these)

1. **Cold Meta ≠ sales at our scale.** At under 30 lifetime orders, the pixel can't run a cold *sales* campaign. Judge cold on CPC + followers, not ROAS. (Crop Tee: 4.25% CTR, $0.13 CPC, +13 followers, **0 orders** — full numbers in Crop Tee Launch — Ad Results & Post-Mortem.)
2. **Sales ads point at the PDP, never the profile or homepage.** Crop Tee leaked ~80% of clicks (204 clicks → 40 landing-page views) because traffic landed on the IG profile.
3. **Retargeting (warm) is where conversions live.** Shift sale budget there first.
4. **Reuse winning creative.** The high-CTR Crop Tee asset is a keeper — re-run with a conversion objective before testing anything new.
5. **Check the plumbing before blaming creative.** Pixel firing + mobile site load speed are the usual cause of a click-to-landing drop.
6. **Capture buyer contact at every pop-up checkout** — IG handle minimum, email if offered. Pop-up 1 captured none, so when the sale mechanic changed same-day there was no direct line to those buyers. Non-negotiable from Pop-up 2 onward.
7. **Email list is ~2% open** — built via a 10%-off lead magnet that attracts discount-hunters. Broadcasts are list hygiene; flows convert. Fix is a re-engagement prune + a better lead magnet (planned, not yet done).
8. **Gifting alone doesn't earn posts in SG.** Use commission (affiliate) or payment (UGC) — not free product hoping for a tag.
9. **2-person team, can't film themselves, not strong at editing.** Every plan must be executable as Canva + phone photos + CapCut, with paid UGC filling the live-video gap.

## 🔁 Recurring rhythms

- **Pop-up 3-job framework:** *Sell* (discount live, sizing visible, card ready) · *Capture* (QR for IG + email, log handles at checkout) · *Content* (5–10 min raw clips, hand-written note in every bag).
- **Sale philosophy:** tier-mechanic-led ("buy more, save more"), never discount-led ("everything on sale" reads desperate). Free shipping no-minimum is the recurring headline lever. Hand-written founder note in every sale order.
- **Long-sale shape (>5 days):** two-peak — launch weekend → quiet midweek (low intensity) → final-weekend push. Don't try to keep it hot the whole way through.
- **Launch-day ops:** Founder A = Launch Ops (discounts/ads/email/metrics), Founder B = Customer & Content (Stories/DMs/notes), time-boxed stagger.
- **Content cadence (normal month):** ~6 carousels (1–2/week) · 4–5 Stories/week (heavier during sale + pop-ups) · 4–5 Reels (UGC + photo-based) · feed posts reserved for genuine moments (sale launch, drops).
