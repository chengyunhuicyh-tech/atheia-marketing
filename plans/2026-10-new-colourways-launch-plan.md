## TL;DR — the recommendation

**Don't wait for the model shots before you start selling, and don't treat flat shots as the launch either. Split it into two moments:**

| Moment | Date | Creative | Who it's for | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tease** | Thu 1 – Fri 9 Oct | Stock unboxing, flat lays, fabric close-ups | Warm IG audience | — |
| **Early Access drop** | **Sat 10 Oct (10.10)** | Flat shots + existing on-model fit shots of the *same cut* | Early-access list, IG Close Friends, past buyers, BFT | Full price, plus **free shipping with no minimum for 48h** |
| **Photoshoot** | Sun 25 Oct | — | — | — |
| **Hero launch** | **Sun 1 Nov** (fallback Thu 5 Nov) | New on-model photos + BTS Reels | Everyone (feed post, Meta retargeting, broadcast) | Full price |
| **First promo moment** | **Wed 11 Nov (11.11)** | On-model photos + first reviews | Everyone | Tier mechanic ("buy more, save more") that includes the new colours |

**Why this shape:**

1. **These are new colours of existing products, so fit isn't the unknown.** Buyers already know the cut. The new photos have to sell the *colour*, not the fit. Flat shots plus close-ups do that well enough for a warm audience, and your existing on-model shots already answer "how does it fit?"
2. **Stock that waits 4 weeks costs you cash, and you gain nothing from waiting.** An early-access window turns those 3 weeks into revenue, email sign-ups and reviews.
3. **Early access gives you a better lead magnet than 10% off** (learning #7). Signing up for first access to a limited colour attracts people who want the product. A discount code attracts discount hunters. This is the list-quality fix that's already planned.
4. **Launching the hero moment on 1 Nov lets 11.11 do the converting.** The hero launch builds awareness, and 11.11 turns it into sales 10 days later. By then you'll have reviews from early-access buyers (Judge.me) to use as social proof.
5. **Don't launch *into* 11.11.** If the colours first appear at a discount, full price looks fake afterwards. They get a month at full price first (10 Oct – 10 Nov).

---

## Theme

**October:** Early Access, not a launch. Tease the colours, open them to the warm audience at full price on 10.10, collect sign-ups, reviews and try-on content. Instagram Stories and IG Close Friends do most of the work. Nothing cold.

**November:** Hero launch on 1 Nov with the new on-model photos, then a tier-led 11.11 sale ten days later, with the photos reused for Black Friday (Fri 27 Nov).

## Phase-by-phase plan

### Phase 0 — Prep (now → Wed 7 Oct)

- [ ] **Confirm stock ETA and allow a 2-day QC buffer.** Early Access on 10 Oct depends on stock being received, QC'd and counted by **Wed 7 Oct**. If it slips, move Early Access to **Sat 17 Oct** and keep everything else the same.
- [ ] **Flat-shot kit (phone + Canva, no studio needed):**
  - Flat lay per colour on Bone Cream backdrop, plus one with all three colours together
  - Fabric macro (texture, stretch, weave) — this is the **Breeze fabric** proof
  - **Colour-matching shot:** new colour next to existing pieces ("pairs with Nimbus / Mocha / Rouge")
  - Hanger shot in natural window light (reads warmer and more lifestyle than a flat lay)
  - A light "SG context" object in the frame, e.g. kopi cup, yoga mat, MRT card (*From studio to kopi*)
  - Apply the warm filter (Warmth +15, Sat −10, Bright +5)
- [ ] **Shopify PDPs (draft, hidden until 10 Oct):**
  - Add the new colour as a **variant on the existing product** if that's how the store is set up. That way the existing on-model images and **existing reviews** stay on the page. If they're separate products, add a line *"Fit shown in [existing colour]: same cut, same BreezeX fabric"* with those images below the flat shots.
  - Add a "Limited first run" note only if quantities really are limited. Don't fake scarcity.
- [ ] **Early-access sign-up** (Shopify customer form or a simple landing section, tag `early-access-oct26`). Wording: *"First access to three new colours, 10.10. No discount, just first pick."*
- [ ] **IG Close Friends list** for early access (DM keyword **"EARLY"** → add to Close Friends and ask for an email). Close Friends Stories are where early-access sneak peeks go.
- [ ] **Book the 25 Oct shoot shot list** (see *Photoshoot plan* below). The shoot has to supply November, 11.11, Black Friday and December gifting, not just the launch.
- [ ] **Check the plumbing** (learning #5): pixel firing on the new variants, mobile load speed on the PDPs.

### Phase 1 — Tease (Thu 1 → Fri 9 Oct)

Goal: grow the early-access list, not sales.

| Day | Instagram | Other |
|---|---|---|
| Stock lands | Stories: raw unboxing, founder voice (hands only, no face needed), *"they're here"* | — |
| +1 | Stories poll: *"Guess the colour"* with a cropped fabric macro | — |
| +2 | **Carousel 1:** *"3 new colours, built for SG heat"*, a blurred/cropped reveal leading to early-access CTA | Sign-up form live |
| +3–5 | Stories: one colour hinted per day, sticker CTA "DM EARLY" | — |
| Fri 9 Oct | Stories countdown sticker → 10.10 | Pre-schedule Early Access email |

- **Cadence:** 4–5 Stories/week rising to daily in the last 3 days, 1 carousel, no feed launch post yet.
- **Meta:** leave retargeting running as normal. **No cold ads on flat shots.**

### Phase 2 — Early Access (Sat 10 Oct → Sat 31 Oct)

Goal: first sales, first reviews, first try-on content.

**Early Access day (Sat 10 Oct, 10.10):**
- Products go live at **full price** with **free shipping, no minimum, for 48h**. That's the usual headline lever, and it's time-boxed so it isn't a discount.
- **Broadcast #1 (plain-text founder voice):** *"You asked first, so you get first pick."* Link straight to the PDP.
- Close Friends Stories first (10am), public Stories 2h later (12pm). The early-access list gets a real head start.
- Hand-written founder note in every early-access order, asking them to *"send us a photo in it — we'll feature you."*

**Rest of October:**
- [ ] **Carousel 2:** Colour story, e.g. *"Why [Colour A]? Designed for the 7am East Coast run"* (lifestyle hook)
- [ ] **Carousel 3:** *"How to style it"*, pairing the new colour with existing pieces (flat-lay grid). This carousel is aimed at cross-sell and bundles.
- [ ] **Carousel 4:** Fabric education, e.g. *"Same BreezeCool, new colour, SGD $X less than Lulu"* (*No brand markup*)
- [ ] **Stories:** repost every customer photo/DM (with permission). Early buyers make better social proof than you can.
- [ ] **Meta retargeting:** swap in the flat-shot + fabric-macro creative for the new colours, aimed at warm audiences only (IG engagers, site visitors, past buyers). **Destination = PDP.** No campaign restart, creative swap only.
- [ ] **Judge.me:** make sure the review request fires for the new variants (~7–10 days after delivery, so reviews start landing late Oct / early Nov, in time for 11.11).
- [ ] **BFT pop-up (if one can be booked in Oct):** this is the best way to cover the on-body gap. People try it on in person, so no photos are needed. Run the 3-job framework and **capture contacts at checkout** (learning #6). Film 5–10 min of raw clips of the rack and try-ons for Reels.
- [ ] **Optional: paid UGC creator (conditional, SGD $150–250).** Send stock on ~12 Oct for 2–3 try-on Reels, delivered ~20 Oct. This covers the 2-week gap before the shoot with real on-body video, and fixes the "can't film ourselves" constraint. **This is the one KIV item worth switching on this month.** Try-on video is exactly what's missing until 25 Oct.

### Phase 3 — Photoshoot (Sun 25 Oct) → edits (Mon 26 – Thu 29 Oct)

- Post **BTS Stories on the day** (the founders film the models and set, not themselves, so the constraint doesn't apply). Set up *"new photos dropping 1 Nov"* as a second reveal.
- **Edit deadline: Thu 29 Oct.** If selects and edits aren't back by then, move the hero launch to **Thu 5 Nov**. Don't launch half-edited.
- Swap the model shots into the PDPs **before** they go into any ad or email.

### Phase 4 — Hero launch (Sun 1 Nov)

This is the "proper" public launch, now with the creative it deserves.

- [ ] **Feed post:** hero image of all 3 colours on models in an SG location. This is a genuine drop, so it earns the feed.
- [ ] **Carousel 5:** *"Now on body"*, with on-model per colour + a final slide with early-access reviews/quotes
- [ ] **Reel:** shoot BTS cut in CapCut (vertical clips from the shoot, see shot list)
- [ ] **Broadcast #2 (Nov):** *"The new colours, finally on body."* Link to the PDP.
- [ ] **Meta retargeting:** swap to on-model creative. Re-use the **format** of the winning high-CTR Crop Tee asset (learning #4) with the new colour. Objective: conversions, destination PDP.
- [ ] **Optional cold test (small, ~SGD $3–5/day for 5 days):** on-model creative, judged **only on CPC + followers** (learning #1). Its job is to fill the retargeting pool before 11.11, not to sell.
- [ ] Update homepage banner + collection image to the new photos.

### Phase 5 — 11.11 (Wed 11 Nov) → Black Friday (Fri 27 Nov)

- **11.11 mechanic: tier-led, whole range, new colours included.** For example: *"Buy 2, save X · Buy 3, save Y"* plus free shipping with no minimum. Make the pitch **"complete the set"**, pairing a new colour with an existing favourite. That puts the new colours into bigger baskets without being a flat markdown on them.
- The new colours have already had a month at full price (10 Oct – 10 Nov), so this doesn't undercut the launch.
- Shape: short sale (≤5 days) → single peak. If it's longer, use the two-peak shape.
- Broadcasts: 11.11 launch + last call. That counts as the November pair, with the hero launch as a 3rd November send. Everything else goes through Stories + Meta retargeting.
- **Black Friday (Fri 27 Nov):** reuse the 25 Oct shoot. No new production needed.

## Photoshoot plan (Sun 25 Oct) — shoot for 3 months, not 1 launch

The shoot is the only on-model content until the next one, so it needs to cover Nov–Dec.

| Need | Shots | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Hero launch | All 3 colours together, 1 SG location | Feed post, homepage, carousel 5 |
| PDP | Front / back / side / detail per colour, plain background | Shopify PDPs |
| Fit proof | Movement shots: squat, stretch, reach (shows support + no see-through) | PDP, ads |
| Pairing | New colour + existing colourways styled together | 11.11 "complete the set" |
| Lifestyle | *Studio → kopi* sequence: class, then kopi / MRT / void deck | Carousels, Reels |
| Ads | **4:5 and 9:16 crops** of the top 3 images, with space for text | Meta retargeting, Stories |
| Video | **10–15 vertical clips**, 3–8 sec each (walking in, turning, fabric stretch, laughing between takes) | CapCut Reels in Nov–Dec. Covers the "can't film ourselves" gap |
| Gifting | Flat lay with the hand-written note + packaging | December gifting |

Ask the photographer to shoot vertical video between setups. That gives you most of a month's Reels at no extra cost.

## The 5 priorities if things get tight

1. **Get the PDPs right**: flat shots + existing fit shots + the "same cut" line. Everything else points here.
2. **Early Access on 10.10**: list sign-ups, Close Friends, one broadcast.
3. **Make the 25 Oct shoot cover 3 months** (vertical video especially).
4. **Hero launch on 1 Nov** with model shots swapped into PDPs, ads and flows.
5. **11.11 tier sale** using early-access reviews as proof.

Everything else amplifies these 5.
