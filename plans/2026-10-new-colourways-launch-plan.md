## The decision: launch now with flat shots, or wait for model photos?

### Recommendation: **launch now (list publicly Sat 10 Oct), then relaunch on 1 Nov with the model photos.**

Treat 1 Nov as a **second launch**, not a photo update. You get two "new" moments instead of one.

### Why launching now wins

| Factor | Launch now (10 Oct) | Wait (1 Nov) |
|---|---|---|
| **Audience size** | Small audience, so there's no big-bang moment to protect. Waiting doesn't store up demand you don't yet have | Waits for a "moment" that a small audience can't make big |
| **Selling time** | ~12 weeks to sell 150 units before year-end | ~8 weeks. You lose a third of the window |
| **BFT, 18 Oct** | Members can buy in person *and* go home and order the colour or size they didn't pick up. Online listing catches the second purchase | Anyone who didn't buy on the day can't find the colours online for 2 weeks, so that interest is lost |
| **Touchpoints (Rule of 7)** | Oct tease + listing + BFT + Nov relaunch = 4 rounds of attention on the same products | 1–2 rounds |
| **Fit uncertainty** | Low. These are new colours of cuts people already know, and the existing on-model shots show the fit | — |
| **Social proof by November** | Reviews + customer photos from October buyers are ready for the 1 Nov relaunch | Launches with zero reviews on the new colours |
| **Cash** | Stock starts turning into cash straight away | 150 units sit in storage for 4 weeks |

### What you give up by launching now

1. **Flat shots convert worse than model shots.** *Mitigation:* nothing cold runs on them. Warm Stories and retargeting only, where people already know the brand and fit.
2. **The first impression is flat-lay, not the hero image.** *Mitigation:* **no feed launch post in October.** Use Stories + 1–2 carousels. The feed hero post waits for 1 Nov.
3. **1 Nov feels less "new."** *Mitigation:* with a small audience, most people won't have seen October's Stories. For them the model photos *are* the launch. For those who did, "finally on body" is still news.

### When waiting is the right call instead

Wait for 1 Nov **only if** the flat shots of a colour genuinely don't look good. **Chiffon Yellow is the one to check**, because soft yellows often wash out or look off on camera. Test-shoot it in natural window light as soon as stock lands.
- If the yellow looks weak but the others look good: list **Glacier Blue + the tank on 10 Oct**, and let **Chiffon Yellow debut in person at BFT** and online on 1 Nov with the model photos. That staggers the colours and gives November a genuinely new product.

## Phase-by-phase plan

### Phase 0 — Prep (now → Wed 7 Oct)

- [ ] **Name the tank colour.**
- [ ] **Stock QC by Wed 7 Oct.** If it slips, list online the day after QC. BFT (18 Oct) stays fixed.
- [ ] **Set aside BFT stock** (~15 per colourway across the full size run, via a separate Shopify location or reduced online inventory).
- [ ] **Flat-shot kit (phone + Canva):** flat lay per colour on Bone Cream · all three together · **tank-over-bra outfit flat lays** · fabric macros (long line band, straps, texture) · hanger shots in window light · pairing with Nimbus / Mocha / Rouge · SG props (kopi, mat, MRT card). Warm filter on everything. **Check Chiffon Yellow's colour accuracy first.**
  - *Optional:* founder mirror shots from the neck down, phone covering the face, for on-body fit without anyone appearing on camera.
- [ ] **Shopify:** add the new colours as **variants of the existing Long Line Bra and Tank** so the existing model shots + reviews stay on the page. If they're separate products, add *"Fit shown in [existing colour]: same cut, same fabric."* Add *"Limited run: 50 made"* to each new variant.
- [ ] **BFT asset pack to BFT by Mon 12 Oct:** 1 flat-lay graphic + *"Atheia's back on Sun 18 Oct with three new colours — only 50 made of each. Try them on after class."*
- [ ] **Check the plumbing** (learning #5): pixel on the new variants, mobile PDP speed.

### Phase 1 — Short tease (Wed 7 → Fri 9 Oct)

A small audience doesn't need a long build-up. Three days is enough.

| Day | Stories |
|---|---|
| Wed 7 | Raw unboxing (hands only): *"they're here 🤍"* |
| Thu 8 | Colour-guess poll (fabric macro crop) + name poll for the tank. People who vote feel invested |
| Fri 9 | *"Tomorrow, 10am"* countdown sticker, one colour revealed |

### Phase 2 — Listed (Sat 10 → Sat 17 Oct)

- **Sat 10 Oct, 10am:** variants live at **full price + free shipping with no minimum for 48h** (ends Mon 12 Oct, 10am).
- **Carousel 1:** *"3 new colours. 50 of each."* One slide per colour (flat lay + fabric macro) → outfit slide → *"Shop via link in bio"* → PDP.
- **Broadcast #1 (plain-text founder voice, to the whole list):** *"We made 50 of each colour. Here they are."* Link to the PDP.
- **Meta retargeting:** creative swap to the flat-lay + outfit set, warm audiences only. **Destination = PDP.** No cold.
- **No feed launch post.** It waits for 1 Nov.
- Hand-written note in every order: *"Send us a photo in it and we'll feature you 🤍"*.
- **Carousel 2 (mid-week):** *"One tank, two moods"*.
- Stories: repost customer DMs/photos, stock updates when true, *"see them in person at BFT on Sunday"*.

### Phase 3 — BFT pop-up (Sun 18 Oct) ⭐

| Job | This pop-up |
|---|---|
| **Sell** | New colours at the front, **hung as outfits** (tank over bra, same hanger). Full size run visible. Usual member perk if you normally offer one (member-only event, so it doesn't undercut the public price). Card reader ready. |
| **Capture** | QR to IG + email. **Log handles and emails for every buyer** (learning #6). **QR to the PDP** too, for *"didn't buy today but want it"*. This is the main reason to list before BFT. |
| **Content** | With consent, film 5–10 min of members trying on and moving. Mirror-selfie-style photos. Hand-written note in every bag. |

**Say this to members at the stand:** *"Brand-new colours, only 50 of each. Same Long Line Bra / Tank you know. The tank's designed to go over the bra. And they're online if you want another colour later."*

**Mon 19 – Wed 21 Oct:** BFT recap Stories · **Reel 1** from the try-on clips (your first on-body video) · thank-you DMs to everyone captured · move unsold BFT stock back online · put the BFT clips into retargeting.

### Phase 4 — Photoshoot (Sun 25 Oct) → edits (by Thu 29 Oct)

- BTS Stories on the day (film the models, not yourselves): *"on-body photos dropping 1 Nov."*
- **Edit deadline Thu 29 Oct**, or the relaunch moves to Thu 5 Nov.
- Model shots go into the PDPs before any ad or email uses them.

### Phase 5 — Relaunch: "On body" (Sun 1 Nov)

- [ ] **Hero feed post** (the first feed post for these colours): all three on models, tank layered, SG location.
- [ ] **Carousel 3:** *"You've seen them flat. Here they are on body."* Per-colour model shots → October customer quotes/reviews → stock-left slide (if true).
- [ ] **Reel 2** from the shoot's vertical clips.
- [ ] **Free shipping with no minimum, 48h** (1–3 Nov).
- [ ] **Broadcast #2:** *"Finally on body"* + honest stock left. PDP link.
- [ ] **Meta retargeting → on-model creative**, reusing the winning Crop Tee *format* (learning #4). Conversions → PDP.
- [ ] **Optional small cold test (1–8 Nov, SGD $3–5/day):** on-model creative, **judged on CPC + followers only** (learning #1).
- [ ] Homepage banner + collection images updated.

### Phase 6 — Quiet period, then low-stock push (9 → 29 Nov)

- **9–12 Nov:** minimum Meta spend, Stories only. Full-price messages get lost in 11.11 noise, and ad costs are high.
  - *Optional (only if you're also skipping Black Friday):* *"No 11.11 sale here. We don't mark up to mark down."* (**No brand markup**)
- **From 15 Nov:** **Carousel 4** *"What you're saying"* (reviews + customer photos) · **Carousel 5** *"3 ways to wear the Eucalyptus tank"* · **Reel 3** · honest low-stock Stories · retargeting back to normal with review-led creative.
