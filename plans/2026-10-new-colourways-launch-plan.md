### Why this shape (updated)

1. **Having only 50 of each is the main selling point now.** 150 units is a small, *real* limited run, so you can say it openly: *"We made 50 of each. That's it."* Scarcity works best when it's true. It gives early access a reason to exist and gives people a reason to buy at full price without a sale.
2. **With no 11.11, the launch has to sell at full price, so the timing depends on social proof.** Early Access (10 Oct) → BFT (18 Oct) → shoot (25 Oct) → hero launch (1 Nov). Each step produces reviews, customer photos and try-on clips for the next one.
3. **The BFT pop-up replaces the paid creator.** Eight days after early access, it's the one place people can try the pieces on before the model photos exist. It's also where you get real on-body video of members, with their consent.
4. **Stay quiet around 11.11.** Every brand in SG is discounting between 9 and 12 Nov. Ad costs go up and a full-price message gets lost. Keep only a low level of retargeting running and pick up again from 15 Nov.
5. **150 units is a lot relative to the ~25–30 lifetime orders you had in June.** Plan for sell-through across Oct–Dec, not a one-weekend sell-out. That's why there's a low-stock push after the launch, and why the Black Friday question below matters.

## Positioning per product

The long line bras and the tank are designed to be worn together, so sell them as outfits as well as individual pieces.

| Piece | Colour angle | Hook it ladders to | Pairs with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Long Line Bra — Glacier Blue** | *"The coolest thing you'll wear in 32°C"*. Icy and fresh, made for the heat | Built for the SG heat / *Feel the difference* | Nimbus pieces, Eucalyptus tank |
| **Long Line Bra — Chiffon Yellow** | *"7am East Coast sunrise"*. Soft, sunny, happy | *From studio to kopi* | Mocha pieces, Eucalyptus tank |
| **Tank — Eucalyptus** | The everyday layer: pull it over either bra and go from class to kopi | *From studio to kopi* | Both new bras, all existing bottoms |

**Main outfit story:** *"One tank, two moods"*: Eucalyptus tank over Glacier Blue, then over Chiffon Yellow. Use this for carousels, the shoot and the hero image. It also encourages 2-item baskets without a discount.

## Phase-by-phase plan

### Phase 0 — Prep (now → Wed 7 Oct)

- [ ] **Name the tank colour** (see above).
- [ ] **Confirm stock arrives and QC is done by Wed 7 Oct.** If it slips, move Early Access to **Sat 17 Oct** and make BFT on 18 Oct the "first in person" moment the next day.
- [ ] **Allocate stock before anything goes live.** Set aside the BFT pop-up allocation in Shopify (a separate location, or reduce the online inventory) so early access can't sell out sizes before 18 Oct. Suggested: **~15 per colourway for BFT** across the full size run, ~35 online. Adjust using BFT sales history.
- [ ] **Flat-shot kit (phone + Canva):**
  - Flat lay per colour on Bone Cream, plus a *"three together"* shot
  - **Outfit flat lays**: the tank laid over each bra
  - Fabric macros (texture, stretch, the long line band, straps)
  - Hanger shots in natural window light
  - Pairing shots with existing Nimbus / Mocha / Rouge pieces
  - SG context props (kopi cup, yoga mat, MRT card). Warm filter on everything.
  - **Optional, only if comfortable:** founder mirror shots from the neck down, phone covering the face. These show on-body fit without anyone appearing on camera. Skip if it doesn't feel right.
- [ ] **Shopify PDPs (hidden until 10 Oct):** add the new colours as **variants of the existing Long Line Bra and Tank** so the existing model shots and reviews stay on the page. If they're separate products, add *"Fit shown in [existing colour]: same cut, same fabric"* and those images. Add *"Limited run: 50 made"* to each new variant.
- [ ] **Early-access sign-up** (Shopify form, tag `early-access-oct26`): *"We made 50 of each. Get first pick on 10.10."* This replaces the 10%-off offer as the way to join the list (learning #7).
- [ ] **IG Close Friends list** for early access (DM **"EARLY"** → add to Close Friends and ask for an email).
- [ ] **BFT asset pack due to BFT by Mon 12 Oct** (BFT runs member comms, you supply the assets): 1 flat-lay graphic + 2 lines of copy: *"Atheia's back on Sun 18 Oct with three new colours — only 50 made of each. Try them on after class."*
- [ ] **Check the plumbing** (learning #5): pixel on the new variants, mobile PDP speed.

### Phase 1 — Tease (Thu 1 → Fri 9 Oct)

Goal: early-access sign-ups, not sales.

| Day | Instagram |
|---|---|
| Stock lands | Stories: raw unboxing (hands only), *"they're here 🤍"* |
| +1 | Stories poll: *"Guess the colour"* with a cropped fabric macro |
| +2 | **Carousel 1:** *"3 new colours. 50 of each."* A cropped reveal of each colour. The last slide is the early-access CTA |
| +3 | Stories: **name reveal** for the tank (or a poll between 2 names, which gets people invested in the product) |
| +4–6 | Stories: one colour per day, "DM EARLY" sticker |
| Fri 9 Oct | Countdown sticker → 10.10, Close Friends preview of the full set |

- No feed post yet. **No cold ads on flat shots.** Retargeting keeps running as normal.

### Phase 2 — Early Access (Sat 10 → Sat 17 Oct)

- **Sat 10 Oct:** variants go live at **full price + free shipping with no minimum for 48h** (ends Mon 12 Oct, 10am).
- **Broadcast #1 (plain-text founder voice):** *"We made 50 of each colour. You're seeing them first."* Link straight to the PDP.
- Close Friends at 10am, public Stories at 12pm, so the early-access list gets a real head start.
- **Meta retargeting:** swap in the flat-lay + outfit creative for the new colours, warm audiences only. **Destination = PDP.**
- Hand-written note in every order: *"Send us a photo in it and we'll feature you 🤍"*. Customer photos are how you'll show the pieces on body in October.
- **Carousel 2:** *"One tank, two moods"*: outfit flat lays, tank over each bra.
- Stories: repost every customer DM or photo, plus honest stock updates when a size is running low (*"Glacier Blue in S: 4 left"*). Only post them when true.

### Phase 3 — BFT pop-up (Sun 18 Oct) ⭐ most important day in October

Use the 3-job framework, with the new colours as the focus:

| Job | This pop-up |
|---|---|
| **Sell** | New colours at the front of the rack, **grouped as outfits** (tank on top of bra on the same hanger). Full size run visible. Card reader ready. Usual BFT member perk applies if you normally offer one. It's a closed, member-only event, so it doesn't undercut the public full price. |
| **Capture** | QR to IG + email sign-up. **Log IG handles and emails at checkout, no exceptions** (learning #6). |
| **Content** | **This replaces the paid creator.** With permission, film 5–10 min of members trying on and moving (squat, reach, band close-up). Get a verbal yes on camera, or a quick consent DM, before posting. Take mirror-selfie-style photos of members in the new colours. Hand-written note in every bag. |

**Say this to members at the stand:** *"These are brand-new colours, only 50 made of each. Same Long Line Bra / Tank you know, same fabric. The tank's designed to go over the bra."*

**Follow-up (Mon 19 – Wed 21 Oct):**
- [ ] Stories: BFT recap with try-on clips, *"sold at BFT: X"*. It makes people want what others are buying.
- [ ] **Reel 1:** CapCut edit of the BFT try-on clips. Your first on-body video of the new colours.
- [ ] DM everyone captured at checkout with a thank-you and ask them to tag you when they wear it.
- [ ] Recount stock and move unsold BFT allocation back to online inventory.

### Phase 4 — Photoshoot (Sun 25 Oct) → edits (by Thu 29 Oct)

- BTS Stories on the day (film the models and set, not yourselves): *"new photos dropping 1 Nov"*.
- **Edit deadline Thu 29 Oct.** If edits are late, move the hero launch to Thu 5 Nov. Don't launch half-edited.
- Swap the model shots into the PDPs **before** any ad or email uses them.

### Phase 5 — Hero launch (Sun 1 Nov)

- [ ] **Feed post:** all three colours on models, tank layered over a bra, SG location.
- [ ] **Carousel 3:** *"Now on body"*: per-colour on-model shots, then a slide of early-access and BFT customer quotes, then a stock-left slide (*"Glacier Blue: X left"*, only if true).
- [ ] **Reel 2:** shoot BTS / vertical clips cut in CapCut.
- [ ] **Free shipping with no minimum, 48h** (1–3 Nov). The same honest lever as early access, for people seeing the colours for the first time.
- [ ] **Broadcast #2:** *"You've seen them flat. Here they are on body."*, with the remaining stock stated honestly. PDP link.
- [ ] **Meta retargeting:** swap to on-model creative. Reuse the *format* of the winning high-CTR Crop Tee asset (learning #4). Objective: conversions → PDP.
- [ ] **Optional small cold test (1–8 Nov, ~SGD $3–5/day):** on-model creative, **judged only on CPC + followers** (learning #1). The job is to fill the retargeting pool.
- [ ] Update the homepage banner and collection images.

### Phase 6 — Quiet period, then low-stock push (9 → 29 Nov)

- **Mon 9 – Thu 12 Nov (11.11 week): stay quiet.** Cut Meta to a minimum warm retargeting budget or pause it. No new posts competing with discount noise.
  - *Optional positioning post (only if you're also skipping Black Friday):* *"No 11.11 sale here. We don't mark up to mark down."* That's your **No brand markup** hook. **Don't post it if a Black Friday sale is coming**, because it would contradict you two weeks later.
- **Sun 15 Nov onward:**
  - **Carousel 4:** *"What you're saying"*: Judge.me reviews from early-access orders plus customer photos
  - **Carousel 5:** *"3 ways to wear the Eucalyptus tank"* (with each bra and with existing pieces)
  - Stories: honest low-stock updates by colour and size
  - Reel 3: second cut from the shoot's vertical clips
  - Meta retargeting back at normal budget, with the review-led creative
- **Late Nov:** see the open question on Black Friday.
